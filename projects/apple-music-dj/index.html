Apple Music DJ

An AI Apple Music curator: it profiles your real listening history and builds playlists in your library. An Agent Skill for Claude Code and OpenClaw.

Live demo Source on GitHub

Overview

Apple Music DJ is an Agent Skill: you talk to Claude Code or OpenClaw, and it reads your listening history through Apple's MusicKit API (recently played, heavy rotation, library, ratings and Replay) to build a profile of your taste. From that it makes playlists straight into your Apple Music library with nine strategies, from deep cuts and decade throwbacks to songs-like-X and two-person blends, sequenced to build and wind down rather than shuffled. Nothing in your account changes until you approve it, and a built-in demo with a fictional listener lets anyone try it without an Apple Developer account. The core needs only Python 3.11, with no pip packages. It's published on ClawHub.

Highlights

  • Taste profiling from your real listening history through MusicKit
  • Nine playlist strategies, sequenced with an energy arc
  • Read-only until you approve a change
  • No-account demo mode against a fictional listener
  • Published on ClawHub; works in Claude Code and OpenClaw
