Weather for iOS 26

A SwiftUI weather app for iOS 26 with Liquid Glass design, widgets and Live Activities, plus an Android companion in Jetpack Compose.

Source on GitHub

Overview

A native weather app built to keep up with Apple's platform as iOS 26 arrived. Forecasts come from Open-Meteo, US severe-weather alerts from the National Weather Service, and radar from RainViewer. It has hourly and 14-day forecasts, air quality and UV, Swift Charts graphs, favourites saved with SwiftData, home- and lock-screen widgets, Live Activities and Siri shortcuts, all in the Liquid Glass design. The architecture is MVVM with @Observable and async/await, and there's an Android companion written in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose.

Highlights

  • Liquid Glass design for iOS 26
  • Open-Meteo forecasts, NWS severe-weather alerts and RainViewer radar
  • Widgets, Live Activities and Siri shortcuts
  • Favourites in SwiftData; MVVM with @Observable and async/await
  • Android companion in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose
