VitalSense

Apple Health insights, fall-risk scoring and caregiver alerts: a native iOS app feeding a web dashboard on Cloudflare Workers.

Live demo Source on GitHub

Overview

VitalSense turns Apple Health data into something a family can act on. A native SwiftUI app reads HealthKit and scores gait, balance and mobility for fall risk (using the iPhone's LiDAR sensor or its motion sensors), and when it detects a fall it alerts the people who look after you. The web side is a React 19 dashboard backed by Cloudflare Workers (Hono), with Durable Objects streaming live data between the phone and the browser over WebSockets, and a caregiver view for remote monitoring. It's live at health.andernet.dev.

Highlights

  • Live at health.andernet.dev
  • Fall-risk scoring from gait, balance and mobility, with fall detection and caregiver alerts
  • Gait analysis from iPhone LiDAR depth sensing or the motion sensors, no wearable needed
  • Native SwiftUI + HealthKit app, with CoreML on the device
  • Cloudflare Workers (Hono) and Durable Objects for real-time sync over WebSockets
  • Tested with Vitest, Playwright and XCTest
